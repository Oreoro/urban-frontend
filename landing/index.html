FAQs

Questions, answered

Is Urban Events really free for free events?

Yes. Free events cost nothing to publish or run — no platform fee, no listing fee, no card required.

How do attendees pay?

JazzCash, Easypaisa and bank transfer, all settled in PKR. Payouts reach your account after the event.

Do I need an app to check people in?

No. Door staff open a link on any phone, scan a QR code or search a name and tap. Nothing to install.

Can I put my own branding on the event page?

Yes. Your logo and colours carry across the event page, checkout and the tickets attendees receive.
