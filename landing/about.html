Built for the creators, powered by tech, simplified for you.

Why is it so hard to manage small events without a mess of spreadsheets?

Urban Events was born out of frustration. As former student organizers and event enthusiasts, we spent more time managing registration lists and verifying payments than actually enjoying the events we worked so hard to create.

We realized that while high-end corporate software exists, it's often too complex and expensive for students, society leaders, and independent workshop hosts in Pakistan. We needed something localized, reliable, and accessible.

One weekend, we mapped out the platform we wished we had during our university days. Now, we've built it for the next generation of Pakistani organizers.

Event planning should feel lighter.

That's the idea behind everything we build.
