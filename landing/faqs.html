Got questions? We've got answers.

Everything about fees, payments, check-in and payouts.

What is Urban Events?
Urban Events is a simplified ticketing and event management platform built specifically for students, societies, and small workshop organizers in Pakistan. We take the stress out of registration so you can focus on your event.
Who can use Urban Events?
While anyone can use us, we're optimized for small to medium-scale organizers: university MUNs, student society concerts, professional workshops, and community meetups. If you're tired of spreadsheets, Urban Events is for you.
Is it really free for free events?
Yes! If you're hosting a free event, Urban Events is 100% free to use. No platform fees, no hidden costs. We want to support the community and help you grow.
How do payments work for paid events?
We support local payment methods including JazzCash, Easypaisa and bank transfers. For paid events, a simple 5% per ticket is added at checkout. Free events are always free, and there are no monthly subscriptions.
How do I check in attendees?
Every ticket comes with a unique QR code. You can use any smartphone with the Urban Events dashboard to scan tickets at the door. It's fast, reliable, and works even with spotty internet.
Can I customize my event page?
Absolutely! You can add your own branding, logos, event descriptions, and custom registration fields to match your event's identity.
What if I need help during my event?
Our support team is just an email or WhatsApp away. We understand that events are high-stakes, and we're here to ensure your check-in process goes smoothly.
How do I get my payouts?
Payouts are processed weekly directly to your designated bank account or mobile wallet after the event successfully concludes.
